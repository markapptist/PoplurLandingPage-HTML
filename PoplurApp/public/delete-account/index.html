Delete your Poplur account

You can delete your account in the Poplur app or request deletion here if you no longer have the app. This page and the request form are available without signing in.

Account deletion is permanent. Your account and the data described below cannot be recovered after deletion.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Poplur and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile using the cube, then open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account, enter that account’s password, and confirm Delete account.
  4. Wait for Account deleted. Poplur signs you out and returns to the welcome screen.

If your password is not accepted, deletion has not started. If Poplur says deletion is incomplete, retry to finish. Closing or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

What account deletion removes

  • Your sign-in, profile, profile photo and selfie, and saved account information such as your name, email address and device notification token.
  • Your posts, their location coordinates and radius records, and media you uploaded, including photos, videos, music, voice posts and message attachments.
  • Messages you sent, your comments and engagement records, and associated notification copies. Messages sent by other people remain theirs.
  • Your credit balance and credit transactions, and account-owned business, campaign, generation-job, wallet-pass, call and report records.

After confirmed deletion, the app also clears that account’s saved biometric sign-in, onboarding draft, cached profile media and local WebView storage on the device performing the deletion.

What is retained

  • A private deletion marker associated with the former account ID, recording the deletion state and timestamps. It contains no email address, name or media and prevents delayed updates from recreating the account.
  • If you made a verified in-app purchase, a purchase-receipt fingerprint marked as consumed, so the purchase cannot be redeemed twice. The account and transaction details are removed from that record.

These records are not automatically deleted.

Remove some data and keep your account

You can delete your own posts and remove profile media in Profile or Settings without deleting your account. You can also request removal of those items using the form below.

Request deletion without the app

Use your Poplur account’s email address. Poplur will contact you to verify ownership before processing the request. Submitting this form does not immediately delete anything.

Your email address and the details you provide are sent to Poplur support by email to handle your request. Do not include your password, payment details or other sensitive information.
