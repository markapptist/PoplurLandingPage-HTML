PoplurSafety

Child safety standards

Poplur has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to every post, profile, message, comment and call on Poplur.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.

What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualizes a minor, including images, video, audio, text, drawings and AI-generated or edited media.
  • Grooming: contacting, befriending or manipulating a minor for sexual purposes, or asking a minor for sexual images, meetings or personal information.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or offering, requesting or linking to CSAM anywhere, on or off Poplur.
  • Using Poplur’s location features to find, follow or meet a minor for any harmful purpose.

Poplur is for people 13 and older. Accounts we believe belong to someone under 13 are removed.

How to report in the app

  1. A post: open the post’s comments and choose Report this pop.
  2. An account: open their profile, tap the block button above Message, and choose to report or block them. Blocking hides their posts from you straight away.

Reports are private: the person you report is never told who reported them. Every report alerts Poplur’s moderators.

You can also email apptistinc@gmail.com. Please describe where you saw the content, but do not copy, save or send the material itself.

How Poplur responds

  • We review reports of child safety violations ahead of all other reports.
  • We remove violating content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the Canadian Centre for Child Protection through Cybertip.ca, as Canadian law requires, and cooperate with law enforcement. We preserve the related records for investigators as the law requires.

Child safety contact

Poplur’s designated contact for child safety, including questions from law enforcement and app stores, is Mark at apptistinc@gmail.com.

You can report directly to Cybertip.ca in Canada or the NCMEC CyberTipline in the United States.
